## Surface

Landing and personal study-planning dashboard. Persuade at entry; Operate after sign-in.

## Audience and task

Spanish-speaking students and teachers planning their own study, review, or grading work around an upcoming deadline. The landing must explain Ally and lead to registration; the signed-in surface must make a personal Monday-to-Sunday schedule easy to read and adjust.

## Scope and constraints

Use the existing Django API for auth, user-owned resources, validation, and schedule generation. Keep the calendar as a real time grid. Support narrow screens and keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop. Do not invent Google OAuth, classroom sharing, or client-side scheduling. Treat the NTE page as a craft and atmosphere reference; use original artwork.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A deadline becomes a visible luminous route through the user's week, not a generic planner landing page.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight ocean-blue grounds, restrained cyan and turquoise light, and readable pearl-white type. Thin time-grid lines hold the schedule in place; luminous material belongs to study blocks and meaningful progress states, not every control.

STORY: Show a deadline, then the sessions that lead toward it. After registration, the user sets categories and availability, creates a goal, asks the API to plan it, and reviews or adjusts the returned blocks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An original full-bleed nocturnal-water illustration frames the Ally wordmark and registration action. A large Monday-to-Sunday luminous timetable is the product proof; concise copy names the deadline-to-study-plan job. Keep navigation, signup, labels, and time grid legible above the fold.

FORM: Selected challenger “La estela luminosa” from the bioluminescent nocturnal plankton world; seed: seed:1290c2b4494fa3d1. Translate the reference's cinematic game-site atmosphere into an original, accessible study-planning experience.
